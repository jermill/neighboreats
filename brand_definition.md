# NeighborEats - Brand Definition

## What the App Does

NeighborEats is a hyper-local, peer-to-peer marketplace that connects neighborhood chefs directly with customers in their community. It's a three-sided platform where:

**Core Function:**
- **Chefs** list meals (subscriptions or à la carte) prepared in their own kitchens
- **Customers** discover, subscribe to, or order meals from local chefs within their radius
- **Drivers** deliver orders from chefs to customers
- **You** take a 15% commission on each transaction and maintain the platform

**What Makes It Different:**
Unlike UberEats or DoorDash (which take 30%+ commission), NeighborEats prioritizes **community, transparency, and fair economics**. Chefs keep 80-85% of meal revenue. Drivers earn $4-5 per delivery plus performance bonuses. Customers pay the same or less while directly supporting their local food ecosystem.

**The Experience:**
1. **Customer opens app** → Geofencing finds chefs within 1-5 miles
2. **Customer browses** → Sees chef LIVE streams, ratings, menus across 10 categories
3. **Customer decides** → Subscribes to recurring meals (15-25% discount) or orders à la carte
4. **Customer receives** → Picks up at hub or driver delivers hot, fresh food
5. **Chef earns** → 80-85% of meal price, guaranteed subscribers, real-time payments
6. **Driver earns** → $4-5 per delivery, performance bonuses, weekly payouts

## Who Uses It

### Primary Users

**CHEFS (Meal Creators)**
- **Profile:** Home-based cooks, culinary school grads, experienced home chefs, small food entrepreneurs, fitness coaches, nutritionists, specialty diet experts
- **Age/Skill:** 25-65, cooking skill level varies (beginner to professional)
- **Motivation:** Make extra income, build personal brand, sell excess capacity, transition to food business, escape 9-5 jobs
- **Pain Point:** Want to monetize cooking skills without heavy startup costs (no commercial kitchen, licensing complexity)
- **Size:** 50-200 active chefs per city at scale

**CUSTOMERS (Meal Buyers)**
- **Profile:** Health-conscious, busy professionals, fitness enthusiasts, busy parents, people with dietary restrictions, working remote, quality-over-convenience mindset
- **Age/Income:** 25-55, $50k-$150k+ household income
- **Motivation:** Support local, eat fresh/healthy, convenience, save money vs restaurants, discover new cuisines, build community
- **Pain Point:** Don't have time to cook, want better quality than fast food, want to know who prepared their food, value local economy
- **Size:** 5,000-50,000 active customers per city at scale

**DRIVERS (Delivery Partners)**
- **Profile:** Gig workers, students, part-time earners, people seeking flexible work, supplement income
- **Age/Motivation:** 18-60, want flexible schedule, better pay than Uber/DoorDash, minimal commitment
- **Pain Point:** Need flexible work, prefer higher per-delivery earnings, want fair treatment
- **Size:** 20-100 active drivers per city at scale

### Secondary Users

**ADMIN (You)**
- Single platform manager initially, expanding to team
- Handles platform operations, resolves disputes, monitors quality

## Who Pays

### Revenue Model

**CUSTOMERS PAY:**
- For meals (directly to chefs via Stripe)
- For delivery (flat fee $4-5, optional)
- For subscriptions (recurring weekly charges)

**Example Transaction:**
```
Customer buys $20 meal + $4 delivery = $24 total
Customer pays $24 to Stripe (via card)
```

**CHEFS RECEIVE:**
- 80% of meal price ($16 from $20 meal)
- Auto-deposited to their Stripe account weekly
- Payment is separate from your commission

**DRIVERS RECEIVE:**
- 100% of delivery fee ($4 from $4 delivery)
- Auto-deposited to their Stripe account weekly
- Bonuses for high performance

**YOU RECEIVE:**
- 15% commission on meal + delivery total ($3.60 from $24 order)
- Stripe auto-deposits to your account weekly
- No involvement in chef/driver payments (Stripe handles it)

### Economic Model

**Monthly Revenue Example (100 active chefs, 1,000 weekly orders):**

```
1,000 orders/week × $24 avg order = $24,000 customer spending/week
Weekly commission (15%): $3,600
Monthly commission (4 weeks): $14,400

Breakdown:
- 200 à la carte orders/week: $4,800
  Your 15% commission: $720/week = $2,880/month
  
- 100 subscriptions/week (avg $95/month = ~$22/week each):
  $2,200 subscription revenue/week
  Your 15% commission: $330/week = $1,320/month
  
Total: ~$4,200/month at this scale (growing)
```

### Why Chefs Don't Pay You Upfront

- No onboarding fees (removes barrier to entry)
- No listing fees (no cost to be on platform)
- Only pay commission on actual meal sales
- Fair incentive alignment (you only earn when they earn)
- Transparent, automatic payments via Stripe (no invoicing)
